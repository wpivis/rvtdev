# Contract Review Study

Thank you for taking part. This session takes about 20 minutes.

You will read **two contracts** and tell us what you would flag for a client. Both
documents are fabricated for this study — the companies, the drafting, and the
problems in them are all invented. Nothing here is legal advice, and nothing you
say will be treated as legal advice.

## What you will do

For each document:

1. **Read it the way you normally would.** The document scrolls inside the pane;
   take as long as you need.
2. **Search it** with the box above the pane if that is part of how you work.
   Searching matches clause headings and clause text.
3. **Flag any clause that concerns you** with the *Flag* button beside it, and add
   a short note saying why. If nothing concerns you, say so with the button below
   the pane.
4. **Rate the overall risk** and write a one- or two-sentence recommendation in the
   sidebar.

There is no single right answer and no time limit. We are interested in *how* you
read, not only in what you conclude.

## Please think aloud

**Say what you are thinking as you read**, out loud and continuously: what you are
looking for, what you skim past, what makes you stop. If you go quiet for a while
we may prompt you to keep talking. This is the most useful data we collect, and it
is the part people most often forget to do.

## What we record

- **Your voice**, for the two review tasks only — not during the questionnaires.
- **Your screen**, for the two review tasks only. Your browser will ask you which
  window or tab to share on the next page. Please share this tab.
- **How you move through each document**: where you scroll, which clauses you rest
  on, what you search for, what text you select, and what you flag.

Recording starts when a review task opens and stops when it ends. You may stop
participating at any time by closing the tab.
