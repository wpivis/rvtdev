# About this survey

This is a demonstration of reVISit's webcam recording feature on a phone-shaped,
image-first instrument. The content is adapted from the **Poverty Stoplight**
instrument used by ASPIRE in Cabarrus County, NC — the *Health and Environment*
dimension, Indicator 19 (Household Violence) and Indicator 18 (Addiction).

## What gets recorded

After you grant permission on the second screen, reVISit keeps a single camera and
microphone stream open for the rest of the study. A small mirrored self-view stays
visible so you always know the camera is on. The video and audio are stored next to
your answers and keyed to the same step, so a researcher can line the recording up
with what you were looking at.

You can pause the microphone at any time with the microphone button, and you can
stop entirely by closing this window.

## Photographs

The three photographs on each question are part of the printed ASPIRE indicator
cards. Rights to that photography sit with Fundación Paraguaya and the ASPIRE
program. Where a photograph is not available, the question shows an empty photo
slot rather than a substitute image.
